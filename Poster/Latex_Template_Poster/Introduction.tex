\block[]{Introduction}{\Large
\begin{itemize}
    \item The Hessian matrix is a target of interest, due to its high computational cost and its versatile applications.
    \item Atomistic Features, based on GFN2--xTB\autocite{Bannwarth2019} quantities, are used for the prediction of the target: Hessian matrix elements
    \item The features are permutation and rotaional invariant to achieve a unique mapping onto the target.
    \item  The zero point vibrational energy is used to quantify the precision of the model.
\end{itemize}
